The authoritative day map is **Part 6 of the plan** —
[`00_MASTER_PLAN_GITHUB.md`](00_MASTER_PLAN_GITHUB.md#part-6--the-day-map). It lists every one of the
253 days with its title, its IDs and its `kind`.

This file is the *reverse* index: ID → day, for when you have a concept and need the day that teaches
it. It is regenerated whenever the plan's Part 6 changes.
